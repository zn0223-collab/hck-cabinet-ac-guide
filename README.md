# How to Choose a Cabinet Air Conditioner for Industrial Enclosures

A practical sizing guide for control panels, telecom cabinets, battery energy storage and prefab cabins.

When ambient temperature climbs past ~40C, a fan or heat exchanger is no longer enough. A **closed-loop cabinet air conditioner** (compressor cooling, IP55 sealed) actively holds the inside at 25-35C and keeps dust, moisture and corrosive gas out.

## Sizing by enclosure volume
- Small cabinet (<2 m3): **3000W**
- Medium (2-4 m3): **5000W**
- Large / container / prefab cabin (>4 m3): **7500W**

## Specs that matter
- IP55 protection for outdoor prefab cabins and factory floors
- Stable operation from **-40C to +55C**
- R410A + high-efficiency compressor + microchannel heat exchanger (~30% better efficiency)
- RS485 / Modbus remote monitoring and alarms
- As low as 52 dB; tool-free filter maintenance
